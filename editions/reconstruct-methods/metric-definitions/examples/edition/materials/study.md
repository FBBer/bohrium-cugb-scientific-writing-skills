# Pendulum Period
## Methods
The method records pendulum period with a timer.
Two independent units A and B were tested. Unit A was read twice and unit B once.
## Results
Under quiet conditions, the timer measured 15 s at the small-angle rig.
The reference timer recorded 21 s at the small-angle rig.
## Limitations
Only the quiet condition was observed; the pendulum period outside that condition was not measured.
Run 1: unit A, repeat 1, quiet, 15 s.
Run 2: unit A, repeat 2, quiet, 15 s.
Run 3: unit B, repeat 1, quiet, 21 s.
15 s were recorded over 1 passes.
15 s were recorded over 2 passes.
21 s were recorded over 1 passes.
