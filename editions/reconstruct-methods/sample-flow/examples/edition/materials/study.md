# Temperature Drift
## Methods
The method records temperature drift with a sensor.
Two independent units A and B were tested. Unit A was read twice and unit B once.
## Results
Under steady conditions, the sensor measured 14 K at the heated chamber.
The reference sensor recorded 20 K at the heated chamber.
## Limitations
Only the steady condition was observed; the temperature drift outside that condition was not measured.
Run 1: unit A, repeat 1, steady, 14 K.
Run 2: unit A, repeat 2, steady, 14 K.
Run 3: unit B, repeat 1, steady, 20 K.
Acquisition 1: 14 samples entered and 1 calibration pass was excluded.
Acquisition 2: 14 samples entered and 1 calibration pass was excluded.
Acquisition 3: 20 samples entered and 1 calibration pass was excluded.
