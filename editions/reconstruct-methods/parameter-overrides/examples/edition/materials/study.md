# Beam Deflection
## Methods
The method records beam deflection with a probe.
Two independent units A and B were tested. Unit A was read twice and unit B once.
## Results
Under dry conditions, the probe measured 13 mm at the support span.
The reference probe recorded 19 mm at the support span.
## Limitations
Only the dry condition was observed; the beam deflection outside that condition was not measured.
Run 1: unit A, repeat 1, dry, 13 mm.
Run 2: unit A, repeat 2, dry, 13 mm.
Run 3: unit B, repeat 1, dry, 19 mm.
